## 📌 Executive Summary

I am a **Data Scientist & Data Analyst** dedicated to transforming ambiguous, complex datasets into robust quantitative solutions and actionable commercial strategies. My work bridges rigorous mathematical analysis and practical problem-solving:

- **Data Ingestion & Cleaning:** Cleaning, imputing, and standardizing raw, missing, or inconsistent datasets.
- **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA):** Detecting non-obvious distributions, multidimensional patterns, anomalies, and correlations.
- **Feature Engineering & Modeling:** Structuring features, statistical evaluations, and machine learning pipelines.
- **Insight Synthesis & Visualization:** Creating intuitive, publication-grade visual narratives that guide strategic decision-making.
